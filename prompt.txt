Write one museum label, in English, at most 200 characters, as a museum in the year 3000 would place it beside an ordinary object from today. Pick a fresh object and a fresh angle — not a plastic fork, parking meter, house key, or group chat. The humour must come from the year 3000 misreading the object: the label says something true and absurd about what the thing was for. Read like a real museum label: short, deadpan, straight-faced and confident, one line, no throat-clearing and no explaining the joke. The laugh must land on a first read, inside the line itself.
